Our game

Capy Forest

A bubble shooter about Castanha and Caju, two capybaras bringing the forests of Brazil back to life. Pop fruit across the Mata Atlântica, the Cerrado and the Pantanal, restore each sanctuary, and meet the animals and plants that come home.

For iPhone, iPad and Android. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
